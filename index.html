Services

What we do

Focused, hands-on controls work. No sales layers, no runaround — you talk directly to the integrator doing the job.

SVC-01

Emergency Controls Support

Line down, faulted processor, comms loss, dead HMI. Rapid response to get production running again — available around the clock for critical failures.

SVC-02

PLC Troubleshooting

Systematic fault-finding on Allen-Bradley, Siemens, and other major platforms. I/O faults, logic bugs, intermittents, and drives that refuse to behave.

SVC-03

Commissioning

Startup and validation for new equipment and process lines. I/O checkout, loop tuning, sequence testing, and documentation your team can actually use.

SVC-04

PLC Migrations

Planned upgrades off obsolete processors onto modern platforms — logic conversion, tight shutdown windows, and a rollback plan if things go sideways.

SVC-05

Legacy Systems

PLC-5, SLC 500, aging SCADA, and control systems the OEM dropped years ago. We keep old iron running while you plan the path forward.

SVC-06

SCADA Support

HMI and SCADA screen development, alarm rationalization, historian issues, and operator interface work that makes the process easier to run.

Process

How a call goes

Same sequence every time, whether it's a 2 AM outage or a planned migration.

STEP 01

Call & Triage

You describe the symptoms. We identify the platform, the risk, and whether it's remote-solvable or needs boots on site.

STEP 02

Diagnose

Systematic fault isolation — logic, I/O, comms, power. No parts-cannon guessing at your expense.

STEP 03

Correct

Repair, reprogram, or replace with a clear explanation of what failed and what it took to fix it.

STEP 04

Housekeeping

Updated program files, marked drawings, and notes so your team isn't guessing six months from now.
